Who we are

We're Gen-Z Digital Private Limited, a small team based in Surat, India. We grew up with a phone in hand, not a desktop — so that's what we build for. No waiting rooms, no manuals, no learning curve: open the app and it should already make sense.

That's the whole premise of the company. We build apps for the problems we run into ourselves — not big platforms, just useful software, shipped by a small team that uses what it makes, for a generation that expects software to just work.

We're early. One product is close to launch, another is still being built. Both are listed below.

What we're building

Focus

Pre-launch · waitlist open
A dashcam app that turns the phone you already have into one. It loops recording within your storage limit, with speed, time and GPS burned into every clip. The moment it senses a crash, that clip locks so it can never be overwritten, and a message to your emergency contact is drafted with your exact location — you just check it and send. Number plates are read on your phone, not in the cloud, and nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on backup yourself. About ₹1 a day, with the first month free.

Stock Compare

In development
A screener for Indian stocks that scores each one on five things that actually matter — quality, growth, valuation, trend, and volume — instead of burying you in raw numbers.
