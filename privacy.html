Privacy Policy

Focus · GEN-Z DIGITAL PRIVATE LIMITED · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GEN-Z DIGITAL PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated in India ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the Focus app ("the App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

Focus has no account, no sign-up and no servers. Everything the App records stays on your phone. We do not collect, receive, share or sell your information. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. What the App records on your phone

InformationWhy the App needs itWhere it is stored
Your name (you type it in)To personalise the AppYour phone only
Video recordings (no audio)The App's core dashcam functionYour phone only
Location and speedTo show speed and position on screen and burn them into your recordingsYour phone only; it becomes part of the clip
Motion sensor dataTo detect sudden impacts (crash detection)Processed on your phone; not stored or sent

None of this information is sent to us. We do not collect audio, contacts, photos from your library, advertising identifiers, or browsing data.

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports, which we use only to fix bugs.

3. How we use information

We do not receive your information, so we do not use it. The App uses it on your phone only to provide recording, the speed overlay and crash detection.

4. Sharing

  • We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have it.
  • If you tap Share or Save to Photos on a clip, that clip goes where you choose (for example Apple Photos or Messages), and that app's own policy applies.
  • Apple distributes the App through the App Store.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — to record video of the road.
  • Location (while using the app) — for the speed and position overlay.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you tap "Save to Photos"; the App cannot read your photo library.

You can turn any permission off in iOS Settings. The related feature will stop working.

6. Retention and deleting your data

Loop footage is deleted automatically once it passes your chosen storage limit; saved and protected clips stay until you delete them. You can delete any clip at any time. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your phone.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. We aim to follow applicable Indian data-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Contact us at the address below for any question or complaint.

8. Children

The App is intended for drivers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Recording responsibly

Laws about recording video and reading license plates differ between places. You are responsible for using the App lawfully. The plate reader must not be used to stalk, harass or spy on anyone.

10. Security

Your footage is protected by your phone's own security. Please keep your phone locked with a passcode.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the App.
