Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Focus does not need an account or sign-up. You enter a name once, and everything stays on your phone.

How do I delete my data?
Delete any clip inside the app, or delete the Focus app to remove all of its data from your phone.

Is Focus an emergency service?
No. Crash detection can miss crashes or trigger by mistake. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
